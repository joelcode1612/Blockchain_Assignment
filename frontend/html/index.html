Platform

Everything you need to ship with trust

From escrow funding to automated payouts, Traxen replaces manual trade-finance friction with verifiable, on-chain logic.

🔐

On-Chain Escrow

Funds are locked in a non-custodial smart contract the moment an agreement is created.

📍

Milestone Verification

Payments release automatically as each delivery checkpoint is cryptographically confirmed.

⚖️

Automatic Refunds

Miss a critical deadline and the contract refunds the shipper automatically.

🧾

Full Transaction History

Every deposit, release, and refund is logged immutably for a transparent audit trail.

🛡

Duplicate-Payment Guard

Each milestone can only be paid once — contract-level checks prevent repeat releases.

🤝

Verified Carrier Network

Work only with wallet-authenticated carriers with reputations built from real deliveries.

Process

How Traxen works

From wallet connection to final delivery, every step is enforced by the smart contract.

1

Connect & Register

Connect your wallet and register as a Shipper or Carrier in seconds.

2

Create & Fund Agreement

Shippers define milestones, payout %, and deadline, then lock ETH into escrow.

3

Track Milestones

Carriers fulfil checkpoints; each one is verified and released automatically.

4

Delivery & Payout

Final milestone completes delivery and releases the remaining balance.

Why Traxen
